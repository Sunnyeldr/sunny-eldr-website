Artist Overview

SUNNY ELDR

Sunny ELDR is an independent artist built around raw female vocals, cinematic atmosphere and a modern Nordic visual identity. The project combines dark, rhythm-driven production with a strong performance aesthetic that moves between contemporary stage energy and Nordic-inspired imagery.

The 2026 catalogue includes the albums ELDRHEIM, HJARTA ÚR ELDI and EPTIR THE FLOOD. The current focus track is Ravenfire, featured on ELDRHEIM.
