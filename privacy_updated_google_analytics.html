Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy explains how personal data may be processed when visiting sunnyeldr.com.

1. Controller and contact

This website is operated for the SUNNY ELDR artist project. Privacy-related enquiries can be sent to contact@sunnyeldr.com.

2. Hosting via GitHub Pages

The website is hosted via GitHub Pages. When the website is accessed, technical data such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, date and time may be processed as necessary to deliver, secure and operate the website.

3. Cloudflare

Cloudflare is used for domain/DNS services and for a Worker that supplies selected public social-platform statistics. Cloudflare may process technical request data such as IP addresses and request metadata in connection with these services.

4. Public social statistics

The website displays selected public platform statistics. YouTube statistics are retrieved through a Cloudflare Worker using the official YouTube Data API. Additional platform statistics may be provided through official APIs where available.

5. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 only after the visitor has explicitly consented to analytics. Google Analytics is a web analytics service provided by Google. The measurement ID used on this website is G-35RYZFM7P5.

If consent is given, Google Analytics may process information about use of the website, including page views, interactions, approximate location information, browser/device information and technical identifiers. This information is used to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its content and performance.

Analytics is not loaded before consent is given. If analytics is rejected, Google Analytics remains disabled for that browser until the visitor changes the setting.

6. Consent storage and privacy settings

The analytics choice is stored locally in the visitor's browser using localStorage under the key sunnyAnalyticsConsent. This allows the website to remember whether analytics was accepted or rejected.

Visitors can reopen the consent controls at any time using the Privacy settings link in the website footer and change their choice.

7. Legal basis for analytics

Where the GDPR applies, Google Analytics is used on the basis of consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Consent may be withdrawn at any time for the future through the privacy settings on the website.

8. Google and international data transfers

When Google Analytics is activated, data may be processed by Google entities and infrastructure outside the visitor's country, including locations outside the European Economic Area. Google applies its own contractual and technical safeguards for such processing. Visitors should also review Google's privacy information for current details about its processing practices and transfer mechanisms.

9. External media and links

The website links to third-party services including Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. Their own privacy policies apply when visitors open those services.

The website may also load third-party content such as Spotify embedded players and YouTube preview images. When such content is loaded, the visitor's browser may establish a direct connection to the relevant third-party provider and transmit technical connection data such as the IP address.

10. Technical processing and legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, technical processing necessary to operate and secure the website may rely on legitimate interests under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Contract-related or pre-contractual enquiries may rely on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Consent is requested where legally required.

11. Retention

The website itself does not maintain a general visitor database. Infrastructure and third-party service providers may retain technical or analytics data for periods determined by their own settings, operational requirements and policies.

12. Your rights

Where applicable, visitors may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, data portability and withdrawal of consent, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

13. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the website, analytics setup, APIs, forms, shop integrations or other services change.

This page describes the current technical setup of sunnyeldr.com. It is general privacy information and is not a substitute for individual legal advice.
