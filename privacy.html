Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This policy explains how personal data may be processed when visiting sunnyeldr.com.

1. Controller

The site is operated for the SUNNY ELDR artist project. Full legal contact details and a dedicated domain email will be added once configured.

2. Hosting

The website is hosted via GitHub Pages. Technical data such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, date and time may be processed as necessary to deliver and secure the website.

3. Cloudflare

Cloudflare is used for domain/DNS services and a Worker that supplies selected public social-platform statistics. Cloudflare may process technical request data such as IP address and request metadata.

4. Public social statistics

The site currently displays public YouTube statistics using a Cloudflare Worker and the official YouTube Data API. Additional public platform statistics may be added later through official APIs.

5. External media and links

The site links to third-party platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Video preview images may be loaded from YouTube image servers, which can cause your browser to transmit technical connection data such as your IP address to YouTube.

6. Cookies and analytics

The SUNNY ELDR website currently does not use its own advertising cookies, tracking pixels or web-analytics service.

7. Legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, technical processing necessary to operate and secure the site may rely on legitimate interests under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Contract-related inquiries may rely on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Consent will be requested where legally required.

8. Retention

The site itself does not maintain a visitor database. Infrastructure providers may retain technical logs for limited periods under their own operational and security policies.

9. Your rights

Where applicable, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to complain to a competent supervisory authority.

10. Changes

This policy will be updated when new APIs, analytics, forms, contact functions or other services are added.

This policy reflects the current technical setup and should be reviewed again after additional social API integrations are added.
